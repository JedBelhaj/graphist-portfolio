## Content problems
- **Usage rights**: do your Upwork/direct client contracts actually let you publicly display that work? Some client agreements are exclusive or confidential. This needs checking before any of it goes on the site.
- **Backlog migration**: how does your existing work (past Upwork jobs, 500px uploads, old freelance clients) get into the site once, at launch? This is a one-time problem, separate from ongoing intake.
- **Missing metadata**: for older pieces, do you actually have the client name, category, testimonial, etc., or will some launch with gaps?

## Media problems
- **Inconsistent source files**: your footage/photos vary in resolution, length, codec, aspect ratio depending on the job. What's the bar for "web-ready" and who enforces it?
- **Where the bytes live vs what visitors stream**: video is expensive to store and serve. Master file storage and visitor-facing playback are two different problems, not one.
- **Photo vs video pipelines**: does a photo need the same treatment as a video, or something simpler?

## Ongoing intake problem (this is the real "self-update" problem)
- **Minimum required action from you**: for every future finished job, what's the smallest thing you personally have to do for it to correctly land on the site? Define this precisely, it's the actual thing you're trying to automate.
- **Who decides metadata at that moment**: you manually, or something guessing from filenames/folders?
- **Silent failure**: if the pipeline breaks, how would you know? No platform gives you a "job done" webhook, so nothing tells you it's stuck.

## Site/presentation problems
- **Structure**: one undifferentiated gallery, or split by service/category the way the reference site splits photography vs videography?
- **Positioning**: what's the one message a visitor needs in 5 seconds? Shibamedia's is "we help restaurants sell more with content." You haven't defined yours yet, and it changes copy, homepage layout, even what goes in the hero.

## Booking problem
- **What's actually being booked**: a free discovery call, a paid shoot slot, a paid consult? Each needs different calendar/payment logic.
- **Money at booking time**: deposit required to hold a slot, or just a calendar reservation?

## Testimonials and logos problem
- **Consent**: do clients need to explicitly agree before you name them or show their logo publicly?
- **Who initiates collection**: you asking manually after each job, or a mechanism that prompts you/them?

## Platform/build problem
- **Maintenance appetite**: the real decision under "Wix vs custom" is how much of this you want to babysit long-term vs pay a platform to abstract away.
- **Budget ceiling**: hosting, video CDN, Wix plan tier, domain. This determines which options are even on the table, not the other way around.

## Scope problem
- **What ships as v1**: gallery + contact form is a very different MVP than gallery + booking + auto-testimonials + auto-ingestion. Which of these is actually required to start sending the link to prospects, and which can wait?

Order to resolve: usage rights and scope first, since everything else (what platform, what automation, what budget) depends on those two answers.
