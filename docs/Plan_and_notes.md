## 2. Structure decision

**Multi-page site, not a single long page like Chiba.**

| Page        | Route               | Content                                   |
| ----------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Home        | `/`                 | Short teaser sections (see 3)             |
| Services    | `/services`         | Service groups, toolbox, packages         |
| Work        | `/work`             | Doors to photo/video + recent projects    |
| Photography | `/work/photography` | Full photography showcase                 |
| Videography | `/work/videography` | Full videography showcase                 |
| About Us    | `/about`            | Story, how we work, team, countries       |
| FAQ         | `/faq`              | Placeholder Q&As                          |

Team lives on the About page rather than its own route.

**Nav (from Bailey Anne):** Home · Services · Work (dropdown: Photography, Videography) · About Us · FAQ, then Instagram + Facebook icons and a "Work with us" button. At the very top the bar is full width; once scrolled it animates into a narrower floating pill.

**About page (from Bailey Anne):** the concept only — big two-tone headline, one warm paragraph, a pill button, a person on the right, lots of air. Our own fonts and colours.
